This page compiles key materials for Mechanical Principles, along with exam preparation tips for quick reference.

## Exam Tips

- **Exam source**: Final exams are mainly based on previous Mechanical Design Fundamentals courses from the mechatronics track; overall difficulty is lower.
- **Reference value**: Some essay-type questions are still worth reviewing and practicing.
- **Short answer questions**: The Mechanical Principles exam tends to have more short answer questions — pay extra attention during review.
